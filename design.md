# Design — Spendly

## Genre

Modern-minimal financial workspace.

## Macrostructure family

- App pages: Split canvas with a quiet navigation rail and a high-energy primary action surface.
- Auth pages: Split canvas with editorial introduction on the left and a focused form on the right.

## Theme

- Paper: cool white and pale blue surfaces.
- Ink: deep navy.
- Accent: electric blue, used for primary actions and selected states.
- Supporting accent: violet only as a low-footprint decorative shape.

## Typography

- Display and body: Manrope, normal.
- Numeric: IBM Plex Mono.
- Display tracking: -0.04em.

## Spacing

Use the named spacing tokens in `tokens.css`.

## Motion

No reveals. Hover and focus transitions only.

## CTA voice

Solid electric-blue rounded rectangle with confident, short copy.

## Shared rules

- Use the blue accent sparingly outside primary actions and active states.
- Avoid decorative icons where a clear text label works.
- Keep app surfaces flat with borders; the primary action surface may carry colour.
